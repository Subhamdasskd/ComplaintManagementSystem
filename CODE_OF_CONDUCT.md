# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a place where anyone can learn and contribute, regardless of
experience, background or identity. Treat other people with respect.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct about code and kind about people.
- Assume good faith; ask a question before assuming a mistake.
- Give credit for ideas and work that came from someone else.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a change that does not fit the project's scope.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues or pull requests.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, commit messages, code comments and any space where
someone is representing the project.

## Reporting

Report a breach to `subhamdasskd7808@gmail.com`. Reports are handled privately. Maintainers may
edit or delete contributions, and may block contributors temporarily or permanently for
behaviour they judge inappropriate.
